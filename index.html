Train&Sync Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-20

Data on your device

Train&Sync stores your workout plans, completed sessions, preferences, and personal exercise photos on your device. The app does not require a Train&Sync account. Device backups may include app data depending on your system settings.

Linked images

Linked-image downloads are on by default. When you open a linked image, the app may contact the image host. The host receives connection information such as your IP address and may retain it under its own policy. The request does not include your workout history or personal photos. In Privacy and data, turn off Download link images to stop new requests. You can also clear cached link images.

Exercise videos

The following applies only to app versions that offer an exercise-video download. If your installed version does not offer it, this download request does not occur. When you choose an available download, the app requests the video file from Supabase. The hosting service records technical request information, including your IP address, the requested filename, request time and status, response timing, client connection characteristics, and an approximate city or country derived from the connection. These records form part of the hosting service’s technical logs. They may be retained under the host’s settings and policies; deleting a downloaded file on your device does not delete server logs. The app does not request GPS location for video downloads. Video requests do not include your plans, weights, completed workout history or personal photos. Downloaded videos are stored locally and can be removed in download management without deleting training data. A valid saved video can play without downloading it again.

Exports and backups

You choose where to export or share files. A plan file includes the plan structure, but excludes personal weights, workout history, and photos. A backup can contain saved data and personal photos and is not encrypted by the app. Your chosen file destination may sync the export. Deleting a local item does not delete copies you have exported or copies retained in device backups.

Permissions and support

Photo selection, camera use, and notifications are optional. You can manage permissions in your device settings. When you visit these pages, GitHub Pages logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. If you email support, we use your address and the information you choose to send to respond and investigate the issue.
